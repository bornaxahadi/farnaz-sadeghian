# Real-traffic plan (agent's outreach job)

Goal: bring REAL people who care about interior design to thirdskin.online,
by being useful where they already gather. No fake traffic, no spam.

## How the agent works here
The agent has no social accounts and must not pretend to be anyone. Each day
it RESEARCHES and DRAFTS; Farnaz (or her team) reviews and posts under her
own name. The agent keeps the pipeline in seo/outreach.md.

Daily: find 3-5 fresh opportunities -> write ready-to-post drafts -> add to
seo/outreach.md as "Ready for Farnaz". Weekly: report which sources sent
visitors (use UTM links, see below) and drop what does not work.

## Ground rules (communities ban self-promotion spam)
1. Read each community's rules first. Many ban links or ads: then only help,
   and mention the site in the profile.
2. Be helpful first: answer the question fully in the post. A link is a bonus.
3. Always disclose it is Farnaz's own site/work. Never fake reviews, fake
   accounts, or "I found this great designer" posts.
4. Never copy-paste the same message into many places.
5. Never email strangers in bulk. Personal one-to-one collaboration pitches only.
6. English only: all drafts, pitches and posts are written in English.

## UTM links (so analytics shows what works)
https://thirdskin.online/?utm_source=SOURCE&utm_medium=MEDIUM&utm_campaign=CAMPAIGN
Example: ...?utm_source=houzz&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=dubai-listing

## Channels, starting list (agent verifies and expands)

### A. Listings and profiles (one-time, permanent backlinks + local SEO)
- Google Business Profile (highest value for "interior designer Dubai")
- Houzz professional profile (very high homeowner intent; reviews matter,
  3+ reviews gets far more enquiries). Ask real clients for reviews.
- Pinterest business account: pins of each project, linked to the site,
  with keyword-rich titles (Pinterest is a search engine)
- Behance (3D renders/visualisation portfolio)
- UAE directories: Yellow Pages UAE, Buildeey, Dexigner directory
  (check each for quality and cost before adding)
- Bing Places, Apple Business Connect

### B. Communities (give help, not ads)
- Reddit: r/interiordesign, r/DIYUAE or Dubai subs, r/Dubai (check rules)
- Facebook groups: Dubai expat/home groups, interior design groups
- Quora: answer real questions on decorating, Dubai, Persian style
- English-language interior design blogs and magazines (pitch guest articles)
- LinkedIn: post project case studies, connect with developers and brokers

### C. Collaborations (biggest long-term traffic)
- Guest articles or interviews on interior design blogs/magazines
  (link back to a specific page)
- Dubai real-estate agents, developers, home-staging partners
- Furniture/decor brands Farnaz already works with: ask for a "featured
  designer" link on their site
- Podcasts and YouTube channels about home design
- Press: HARO-style journalist requests, Gulf lifestyle magazines

### D. Content that earns links (agent suggests, Farnaz approves)
- "Dubai apartment layout guides" with 3D plans
- "50 interior styles" series from the book, one page per style
- Before/after case studies (already have images in /img)
- English guides aimed at Dubai/UAE expats and homeowners

## Tracking
Weekly add a row to seo/log.md: source | visits | signups | enquiries.
Stop channels with no result after 4 weeks.
