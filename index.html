    About Farnaz Sadeghian

    A designer's eye, made simple

    “A beautiful home is not about spending more. It's about knowing what to choose.”

    Farnaz has lived in Dubai for twelve years. She trained in interior decoration, earned her certification through TAFE in Australia, and spent the following years designing, consulting and staging real homes. More than fifty Dubai properties were staged by her, each one planned first in 3D.

    That hands-on experience is what her followers learn from today. Every tip is something she has tested in a real room: how to place a sofa in a long living room, how to hide an awkward pillar, what to buy and what to skip.
